# IDTM Installation Description

## Overview
This document describes all installation steps for the **IDTM (Infectious Disease Treatment Module)** based on the AZF4-27 Inflatable Tent system. The IDTM consists of 3 tents (2 patient pods + 1 staff pod) with 4 vestibules, hard flooring, and sun shadowing nets.

**Source Document**: EN-MAN-AZF4-27-WFP_User_Manual_DRAFT01 (1).pdf

## Installation Site Selection and Preparation
**📄 PDF Reference**: Page 15 | Section 3.3 "Installation Site Selection and Preparation"

### Step 1: Site Assessment
**Purpose**: Ensure optimal stability and longevity of the tent system

**Actions**:
1. Verify the installation site is **flat** (maximum slope: 5%)
2. Check for **minimum surface area of 276 m²**
3. Ensure site is at least **2m away** from nearest trees or buildings
4. Verify ground is **compact and dry** enough for pegs

### Step 2: Site Preparation
**Purpose**: Prevent damage to tent materials and ensure proper drainage

**Actions**:
1. **Clean the area** - Remove sharp objects (stones, chippings, branches)
2. **Fill deep recesses** if slope exceeds 5%
3. **Dig drainage trench** around perimeter to prevent water accumulation
4. **Clear debris** that could puncture the groundsheet

**⚠️ Safety Warning**: Never drag tent components over the ground to avoid damage.

### Step 4.5: Connect Electric Inflator

3. Secure manifold using provided fastening pin

### Step 4.6: Prepare Valves for Inflation
**📄 PDF Step Reference**: Page 20, Step 12.0

**📷 Step Images**:
- `images/20_tent_inflation_step_10-13_img6.png` - Valve states diagram (CLOSED vs OPEN)
- `images/20_tent_inflation_step_10-13_img7.jpeg` - Closing valve by turning clockwise
- `images/20_tent_inflation_step_10-13_img8.jpeg` - Verifying valve is closed
**Purpose**: Ensure valves are properly closed before inflation

**⚠️ Critical Check**: All valves must be closed before starting inflation

**Valve States**:
- **CLOSED**: Central spring pin is UP (~1cm below rim) ✓
- **OPEN**: Central spring pin is DOWN ✗

**Actions**:
1. Close all open valves by:
   - Pushing central pin DOWN
   - Turning valve tappet CLOCKWISE
2. Verify all valves are closed (pin should be UP)

### Step 4.7: Connect Inflation Hoses
**📄 PDF Step Reference**: Page 20, Step 13.0

**📷 Step Images**:

- `images/20_tent_inflation_step_10-13_img3.png` - Fastening screw ring
**Purpose**: Establish air connection to tent frame

**Actions**:
1. Connect end of inflation hoses to valves
2. Fasten hoses by turning screw ring **clockwise**
3. Ensure inflation tap is **OPEN**
4. Verify all valves are connected to inflation hoses

### Step 4.8: Inflate Tents
**📄 PDF Step Reference**: Page 21, Steps 14.0-15.0

**📷 Step Images**:
- `images/21_tent_inflation_step_14-15_img4.jpeg` - Inflator ON, tent inflating
- `images/21_tent_inflation_step_14-15_img5.png` - Tent during inflation process
- `images/21_tent_inflation_step_14-15_img6.jpeg` - Fully inflated tent
**Purpose**: Erect tent structure to operating pressure

**⚠️ Important Safety Notes**:
- Open doors during inflation to prevent air passage valve damage
- Pull gable guy ropes during inflation to facilitate erection
- Close unused hose taps for more efficient inflation

**Operating Pressure**: 0.3 bar

**Actions**:
1. Connect inflator to power source
2. Switch inflator **ON**
3. Monitor inflation process
4. **Pressure indicators**:
   - With pressure gauge: Stop at 0.3 bar
   - Without gauge: Stop when inflator sound becomes higher (wait 1 min)
5. Switch inflator **OFF**

**⚠️ Critical Warning**:
- Maximum inflator pressure: 0.45 bar (below overpressure valve threshold)
- Do NOT wait for overpressure valves to open - this may cause overheating
- Never turn off inflator if overpressure valves open

### Step 4.9: Disconnect and Secure Valves
**📄 PDF Step Reference**: Page 22, Step 16.0

**📷 Step Images**:
- `images/22_tent_inflation_step_16-18_img3.jpeg` - Disconnecting inflation hoses
- `images/22_tent_inflation_step_16-18_img5.jpeg` - Screwing protective caps back on
- `images/22_tent_inflation_step_16-18_img7.jpeg` - Closing PVC protective flap
**Purpose**: Seal tent frame after inflation

**Actions**:
1. Unfasten inflation hoses from tent valves
2. Screw protective caps back on (turn clockwise)
3. Close PVC flaps to protect valves from sand/mud

### Step 4.10: Install Pressure Monitoring System
**📄 PDF Step Reference**: Page 22-23, Steps 18.0-21.0

**📷 Step Images**:
- `images/22_tent_inflation_step_16-18_img1.png` - Pressure monitoring coupling on inflator
- `images/22_tent_inflation_step_16-18_img2.png` - Pressure feedback tube insertion
- `images/23_tent_inflation_step_19-23_img3.png` - Feedback tube routing into tent
- `images/23_tent_inflation_step_19-23_img5.jpeg` - Pressure feedback valve inside tent
- `images/23_tent_inflation_step_19-23_img6.png` - Connecting tap to feedback valve
**Purpose**: Enable automatic pressure maintenance

**System Components**:
- White pressure feedback tube
- Pressure monitoring coupling on inflator
- Pressure feedback valve inside tent

**Actions**:
1. Locate white pressure feedback tube in inflator bag
2. Insert free end (no tap) into **pressure monitoring coupling** of inflator
3. Unroll pressure feedback tube
4. Insert other end (with tap) inside tent through:
   - HVAC duct sleeves, OR
   - Inflation valve openings
5. Inside tent, locate pressure feedback valve on **rear pneumatic arch**
6. Open protective cap
7. Insert inflation tap into pressure feedback valve
8. Fasten screw ring and open tap

### Step 4.11: Enable Automatic Pressure Control
**📄 PDF Step Reference**: Page 23, Step 23.0

**📷 Step Images**:
- `images/23_tent_inflation_step_19-23_img7.png` - Inflator display showing AUTO mode and pressure reading
**Purpose**: Maintain proper tent pressure automatically

**⚠️ Critical Warning**: Never switch to AUTO if feedback tube is NOT connected - inflator will detect incorrect pressure and overheat.

**Operating Mode**: AUTO
- Inflator displays current pressure on screen
- Automatically turns ON if pressure drops below 0.2 bar
- Automatically turns OFF when pressure reaches 0.3 bar

**Power Outage Procedure**:
1. Turn inflator to **OFF** immediately
2. Close all hose taps to prevent air loss through inflator

**Actions**:
1. Verify feedback tube is connected to tent frame
2. Turn inflator to **AUTO** mode
3. Verify pressure reading on display screen

**Actions**:
1. Pull on four corners of all 3 tents
2. Straighten groundsheet
3. Avoid folds inside tent

### Step 8.2: System Activation
**Purpose**: Complete installation and begin operational readiness

**Final Actions**:
1. Verify pressure monitoring inflator is in AUTO mode
2. Check all HVAC duct sleeves are properly sealed
3. Verify cable sleeves are sealed
4. Test all doors and windows for proper operation
5. Confirm electrical system is operational (lights, power outlets)
6. Verify all inflation valves are capped and protected
